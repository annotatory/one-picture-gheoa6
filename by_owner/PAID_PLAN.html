5. How each campaign is tested and run

  • Design: 2 pictures × 2 audiences. Audience 1: women 18+ in Hyderabad, broad. Audience 2: women 18+ in a second city, broad (default Bengaluru; your call). No interest targeting by any health condition.
  • Scale: an ad set at or under ₹250 CPR with 10 or more reservations gets 20% more daily budget every 2 days, never above the campaign's approved total (estimate: no cited benchmark; replace with C1's own numbers).
  • Kill: pause an ad set over ₹500 CPR after ₹5,000 spent, or with no reservation after ₹3,000; move its money to the best ad set (estimate: no cited benchmark; replace with C1's own numbers).
  • Promise guard: if 48-hour misses (automatic refunds) pass 5% of a day's reservations, pause every ad and tell you: the promise matters more than the spend. The 5% is an estimate (no cited benchmark; replace it with our own numbers).
  • Pause everything and tell you on: an ad rejection or account warning, a complaint in the comments, a refund complaint, or the stop rule in the kit.
  • Daily send-report while anything runs: spend, landing-page views, reservations, CPR per ad set, refunds, and the counter.
