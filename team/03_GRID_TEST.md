# Grid test: do this before anything goes live

**Why.** On Instagram the 12 posts join into one big picture on the profile. That only works if Instagram trims each picture the way we expect. The plan assumes it. This test checks it on a private account, so nothing public is affected. It takes about ten minutes.

## What you need

- A **private test Instagram account** (Megh gives you one, or you create a new one and set it to **Private**: Settings, then Privacy, then Private account).
- The folder **TILES_post_in_this_order** (in this folder). It holds 12 pictures named 01 to 12.

## Steps

1. On the test account, post the 12 pictures **one at a time, in order: 01 first, then 02, and so on up to 12.** Each is a **single picture post** (not a carousel). Do not add a caption. Do not add filters.
2. After picture 12, open the profile page. Take a **screenshot of the whole grid** (3 pictures across, 4 rows).
3. Compare it with the example **profile_grid_after_12.png** (in this folder).
4. Send your screenshot to Megh with one word: **JOINED** or **NOT JOINED**.

## What "joined" looks like

Look at the middle two rows. There should be **one flower of six pale petals** that carries across the six tiles, with a dashed ring running through them and the small coral dots at the petal tips. The words (Body, Mood, Sleep, Stress, Focus, Energy) each sit inside their own tile. The top row holds "The last piece is you.", the ₹499 call picture and "Nari 1. Women first." The bottom row holds "Six parts of you...", "Everyone's taken care of..." and "Head and hormones. One picture."

**Not joined** means: petals are cut off at the edge of a tile, one tile looks shifted, or a gap or double edge shows between tiles.

## If it is not joined

1. **Do not post anything live.**
2. Tell Megh. Send the screenshot.
3. The picture is re-made (about 30 seconds). You will get new files. Repeat the test with the new files.

## Optional: see how it looks half way

The pictures **profile_grid_after_03.png, _06.png and _09.png** show how the profile looks after 3, 6 and 9 live posts. Between 6 and 12 the ring crosses a tile edge slightly off. That is expected. It is whole at 12.
