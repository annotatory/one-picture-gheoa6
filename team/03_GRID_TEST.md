# Grid test: do this before anything goes live

**Why.** On Instagram the 12 posts join into one big picture on the profile. That only works if Instagram trims each picture the way we expect. The plan assumes it. This test checks it on a **spare private account**, so nothing public is affected and the live account is never touched.

**Who.** The team lead does this, by hand, from a phone. No tool or app is connected to any account.

## What you need

- A **second, spare Instagram account** that Megh has approved for this one test. Set it to **Private** (Settings and activity, then Account privacy, then Private account). It is **never the live account**. Before every step, look at the username at the top of the profile and check it is the spare one.
- The folder **TILES_post_in_this_order** (in this folder). It holds 12 pictures named 01 to 12.

## Steps

1. Open the Instagram app and switch to the **spare** account (tap your username at the top of the profile). Check the username.
2. Post the 12 pictures **one at a time, in order: 01 first, then 02, and so on up to 12.** Each is a **single picture post** (not a carousel). Do not add a caption. Do not add filters. If Instagram shows a warning or stops you, stop, wait, and tell Megh.
3. After picture 12, open the profile page. Take a **screenshot of the whole grid** (3 pictures across, 4 rows).
4. Compare it with the example **profile_grid_after_12.png** (in this folder).
5. Send your screenshot to Megh with one word: **JOINED** or **NOT JOINED**. Wait for Megh's answer.
6. When Megh says **JOINED**: delete the spare account (next section) and tell Megh when it is gone. When Megh says **NOT JOINED**: delete the 12 test posts on the spare account only, wait for new files, and repeat from step 2. Delete the account only after a JOINED.

## How to delete the spare account

**Check the username says the spare account before you tap anything. Never delete the live account.**

In the Instagram app: your profile, then the menu (three lines), then **Settings and activity**, then **Accounts Center**, then **Personal details**, then **Account ownership and control**, then **Deactivation or deletion**. Choose the **spare** account, then **Delete account**, and follow the steps. Button names move between versions of the app: if one is missing, pick the closest one only if you are sure it does the same thing, or search "delete account" in the app's Help, or ask Megh. Instagram may keep a deleted account hidden for a while before it is gone for good: leave it **Private** until then. Take a screenshot of the confirmation and send it to Megh.

## What "joined" looks like

Look at the middle two rows. There should be **one flower of six pale petals** that carries across the six tiles, with a dashed ring running through them and the small coral dots at the petal tips. The words (Body, Mood, Sleep, Stress, Focus, Energy) each sit inside their own tile. The top row holds "The last piece is you.", the call picture (two figures and a dotted line) and "Nari 1. Women first." The bottom row holds "Six parts of you...", "Everyone's taken care of..." and "Head and hormones. One picture."

**Not joined** means: petals are cut off at the edge of a tile, one tile looks shifted, or a gap or double edge shows between tiles.

## If it is not joined

1. **Do not post anything live.**
2. Tell Megh. Send the screenshot.
3. The picture is re-made (about 30 seconds). You will get new files. Delete the 12 test posts on the spare account and repeat the test with the new files.
