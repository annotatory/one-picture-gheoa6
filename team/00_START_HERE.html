What this is. Twelve social posts that lead up to the launch of Nari 1, a women's health app. Each post is a set of pictures (slides) that go out together. On Instagram all twelve go out on one launch day, in one sitting, from post 1 to post 12: there the twelve posts join into one big picture on the profile, which is why the order matters and why they go out together. On Facebook, LinkedIn and X they go out one a day, in the same order, starting on the launch day (post 1 on the launch day, post 12 on day 12), so follower feeds there are not flooded. Posts after the 12 are paced: Megh sends that plan. You are the team lead: you post them by hand from the phone app, exactly as prepared, and you own the two logs (07_POSTED_LOG and 08_ENGAGEMENT_LOG). Megh decides everything else. No tool or app is connected to any account: nobody posts through software.

Dates. Instagram: all 12 posts go out on one launch day, in order 1 to 12, so the profile grid forms the picture at once (Megh, 28 Oct 2026). Planned launch day: Fri 6 Nov 2026. Facebook, LinkedIn and X: one post a day, in order 1 to 12, starting on the launch day (post 1 on Fri 6 Nov 2026, post 12 on Tue 17 Nov 2026), so follower feeds there are not flooded (28 Oct 2026, night). The day waits until every post is cleared: if any post says STOP-UNTIL, nothing goes out, and the launch day moves to the first day all 12 are cleared (the Facebook, LinkedIn and X dates move with it). Posts after the 12 are paced; Megh sets that plan.

The 6 rules

  1. Instagram: all 12 on the launch day. Facebook, LinkedIn and X: one a day. Always in order, 1 to 12. Nothing goes out until every post in 01_POSTING_CALENDAR says GO or GO-FALLBACK: if any post says STOP-UNTIL, post nothing at all. On the launch day, work in rounds: the Instagram feed for posts 1 to 12 first, then Instagram Stories 1 to 12, then post 1 on Facebook, LinkedIn and X. Then one post a day on Facebook, LinkedIn and X: post 2 on day 2, up to post 12 on day 12 (01_POSTING_CALENDAR gives the dates). Never skip a post. Never change the order. Post n goes out only after post n-1 is live on that platform and in 07_POSTED_LOG.csv. If a post fails or gets stuck, stop there and tell Megh.
  2. Post the slides of a post in order. Slide 1 first, then 2, then 3.
  3. Never edit a picture or a caption. Use every file exactly as it is. Copy and paste, do not retype.
  4. Never post anything marked STOP or HELD. If the folder name or the STATUS says STOP, nothing from it goes out. A post is GO only when its own STATUS says GO: the status already counts the start gates and the launch day (every post says STOP while either is open).
  5. If a comment complains, a platform sends a warning, or reach drops: stop and tell Megh before you post again. Details are in 06_COMMENTS_AND_SAFETY.
  6. Not sure? Ask Megh. Do not guess. Megh is the only person you ask.

Things on the pictures that are meant to be there

  • A small pill saying Sample data on slides that show an app screen. The numbers on those screens are example data. Leave it.
  • Posts 4 to 9 say Piece 1 of 6 to Piece 6 of 6. These piece numbers are separate from the post numbers (post 5 is Piece 2). That is correct.
  • On the first slide of posts 4 to 9, shapes are cut off at the edge on purpose. Each first slide is one piece of the big profile picture. Do not crop or "fix" them. Check only that you picked the right file, in the right order.
