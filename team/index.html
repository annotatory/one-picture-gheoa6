The three things to remember

  1. Post in order, 1 to 12, one a day, slides in order.
  2. Never edit a picture or a caption. Never post anything marked STOP.
  3. Not sure, or something looks wrong: stop and ask Megh.

Sign-offs that decide whether a post may go out are tracked on the master tracker and the start sequence. The status you see in the kit folder names is the one to follow.
