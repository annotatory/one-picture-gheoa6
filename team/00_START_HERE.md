**What this is.** Twelve social posts that lead up to the launch of Nari 1, a women's health app. Each post is a set of pictures (slides) that go out together. One post a day, from post 1 to post 12. On Instagram the twelve posts also join into one big picture on the profile, which is why the order matters. You are the **team lead**: you post them by hand from the phone app, exactly as prepared, and you own the two logs (07_POSTED_LOG and 08_ENGAGEMENT_LOG). Megh decides everything else. No tool or app is connected to any account: nobody posts through software.

**Dates.** Planned: post 1 on Wed 28 Oct 2026, one a day, post 12 on Sun 8 Nov 2026 (day 12). That implies a launch around Sun 8 Nov 2026. Any STOP-UNTIL post can push the later days: the grid order never changes, and a STOP halts the series at that post. If a post is held, every later date moves by the same number of days.

## The 6 rules

1. **Post in order, 1 to 12, one a day.** Never skip a post. Never change the order. Before you post today's, check that yesterday's post is in **07_POSTED_LOG.csv**, or that the **Right now** box above says it is live. If neither, stop and ask Megh. If you are a day late, post the missed post that day and carry on: never post two on one day, and tell Megh.
2. **Post the slides of a post in order.** Slide 1 first, then 2, then 3.
3. **Never edit a picture or a caption.** Use every file exactly as it is. Copy and paste, do not retype.
4. **Never post anything marked STOP or HELD.** If the folder name or the STATUS says STOP, nothing from it goes out. A post is GO only when its own STATUS says GO: the status already counts the start gates (every post says STOP while they are open).
5. **If a comment complains, a platform sends a warning, or reach drops: stop and tell Megh** before you post again. Details are in 06_COMMENTS_AND_SAFETY.
6. **Not sure? Ask Megh.** Do not guess. Megh is the only person you ask.
