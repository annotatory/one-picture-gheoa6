# How to post

Read this once before your first post. Replace "P05" with the post number you are posting. If a button has a different name in your version of an app, pick the closest one **only if you are sure it does the same thing**. If you are not sure, stop and ask Megh.

## Which folder goes to which platform

| Platform | Folder to use | Which file |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed (a carousel) | INSTAGRAM_feed_4x5 (tall, portrait) | all the slides, in order |
| Instagram Story | INSTAGRAM_story_9x16 (very tall) | the one story file |
| Facebook | LINKEDIN_FACEBOOK_1x1 (square) | all the slides, in order |
| LinkedIn | LINKEDIN_FACEBOOK_1x1 (square) | all the slides, in order |
| X | X_wide_16x9 (wide, landscape) | the one wide file |

LinkedIn uses the **square** folder (LINKEDIN_FACEBOOK_1x1), not the wide one. The wide folder (X_wide_16x9) is for X only. Copy only the folder for the platform you are posting to. When you pick pictures, pick them from the folder in your Files or Drive app if the platform lets you; if you must use the camera roll, check the shape (tall, square or wide) matches the table before you post.

## The time

**Instagram: all 12 posts on the launch day.** Start at the opening of **7:30 to 9:30 pm India time** and work in rounds: **round 1** the Instagram feed for posts 1 to 12, in order (the grid forms at once; never leave it half done); **round 2** Instagram Stories 1 to 12; **round 3** post 1 on Facebook, then LinkedIn, then X. **Facebook, LinkedIn and X: one post a day** (28 Oct, night: twelve posts in one day would flood follower feeds there). Each day after the launch day, at the opening of the same window, post the next post on Facebook, LinkedIn and X: post 2 on day 2, up to post 12 on day 12 (01_POSTING_CALENDAR gives each date). Never post two on one platform in a day: if a day is missed, tell Megh. The sitting takes about two hours: if the window ends before you finish, finish the round you are in and tell Megh. This window is the default and it is a **test**: it is not based on our own data. Every post gets its 24-hour numbers written in 08_ENGAGEMENT_LOG.csv. After the 12, Megh looks at the log and sets the time for the later, paced posts. Do not move it yourself. Each post's Story goes after its feed post, never before.

## Before the launch day sitting (10 minutes)

1. Open 01_POSTING_CALENDAR. Check that **every** post, 1 to 12, says **GO** or **GO-WITH-FALLBACK** under Status today. If any post says **STOP-UNTIL**, do not post anything. Tell Megh.
2. Open nari1.com and check it still shows founding spots. If all 1,000 are taken, do not post anything: tell Megh. Check again on days 10, 11 and 12, before posts 10, 11 and 12 go out on Facebook, LinkedIn and X: if all are taken by then, do not post them: tell Megh.
3. Open each post folder in 04_POSTS. The folder name ends in the status (for example P05_Focus__GO).
4. Open each STATUS.txt. It has a **card for each platform** with the account, the exact files in the order to pick them, the caption file and a ready log row. If it says GO-WITH-FALLBACK, use the fallback files it names.
5. Copy the files to your phone for all 12 posts: the folder for each platform, plus caption.txt, caption_for_X.txt and alt_text.txt.

## Between posts

Post n goes out only after post n-1 is live on that platform: check it, then add its row to 07_POSTED_LOG.csv, then post the next one (on Facebook, LinkedIn and X: the next day).

## The day after

Do the **24-hour check** for the 12 Instagram posts and for post 1 on Facebook, LinkedIn and X: open each platform's insights for each post and copy the numbers into 08_ENGAGEMENT_LOG.csv (see "The engagement log" below). On Instagram: open the post, tap **View insights**. Then every day until day 13, before you post the next one, do the same for the Facebook, LinkedIn and X post of the day before.

## Instagram feed (a carousel of slides)

1. Open Instagram. Tap the **+** button, then **Post**.
2. Tap the **Select multiple** button (two overlapping squares), then tap the slides **in order: slide 1 first, then slide 2, then slide 3.** Check the little numbers 1, 2, 3 on the pictures. A post with one slide: just pick it.
3. The pictures are already 4:5 portrait. If Instagram offers a crop or format button, choose the **full portrait** version. Do not zoom or move the picture.
4. Tap **Next**. Leave everything alone: no filters, music, stickers, text, hashtags, people tags, product tags or location. Tap **Next**.
5. **Caption:** open caption.txt, select all, copy. In Instagram tap the caption box and paste. Do not change a word.
6. **Alt text:** scroll down, tap **Advanced settings**, then **Write alt text**. For each picture paste the line from the matching block of alt_text.txt (PICTURE 1 for the first picture, and so on). Tap **Done**. If a box cuts a line off, paste what fits and tell Megh.
7. Leave **Audience** alone. **Also share to Facebook** and **Threads** must be **off**: if they are on in your app, switch them off before you share. Do not change any other option.
8. Tap **Share**.

## Instagram Story

1. In Instagram swipe right (or tap **+**, then **Story**).
2. Pick the story file from INSTAGRAM_story_9x16 (P05_story.png). Do not add text, stickers, music or links.
3. If Instagram offers to share the story to Facebook as well, leave that **off**: Facebook gets these posts one a day, not twelve at once. Tap **Your story**. A Story has no caption and needs no alt text. (The same file is what Megh uses for WhatsApp Status. You do not post to WhatsApp unless Megh asks.)

## Facebook (square pictures)

1. Open Facebook. Tap **Photo** in the post box.
2. Pick the slides from LINKEDIN_FACEBOOK_1x1 in order, slide 1 first.
3. Paste caption.txt. If you can edit a photo and see **Alt text**, paste the matching line (the line under PICTURE 1 for picture 1, and so on). If not, skip.
4. Tap **Post**.

## LinkedIn (square pictures)

1. Open LinkedIn. Tap **Post**.
2. Tap the picture button. Pick the slides from LINKEDIN_FACEBOOK_1x1 **in order, slide 1 first.**
3. Paste caption.txt. If an **Alt text** button shows when you tap a picture, paste the matching line. If not, skip.
4. Tap **Post**.

## X (one wide picture)

1. Open X. Tap the new post button.
2. Tap the picture button and pick the wide file from X_wide_16x9 (P05_wide.png).
3. Paste **caption_for_X.txt**. It is the caption cut to fit X. When the caption already fits, the two files are the same, and that is fine. If the picture has an alt-text option (Add description), paste the "Story and wide picture" line from alt_text.txt.
4. Tap **Post**.

## Alt text: how to read alt_text.txt

The file has one block per picture: "PICTURE 1 of 3", then the line to paste on the next line. Paste only that line (the words under the label, not the label). Do not change a word.

## After you post (check each platform)

| Platform | Check | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed | Open the live post. Swipe every slide: order 1, 2, 3, nothing cut off (except the on-purpose crops on slide 1 of posts 4 to 9). Read the caption. Open the three dots, Edit, and check the alt text is there. After post 12, open your profile and take a **screenshot of the grid**; send it to Megh. | Add the log row. |
| Instagram Story | Open your story. Check the picture shows and is the right one. | Add the log row. |
| Facebook and LinkedIn | Open the post. Check slide order and the caption. | Add the log row. |
| X | Open the post. Check the picture and the caption. | Add the log row. |

**The log.** Add one row to **07_POSTED_LOG.csv** per platform, after the checks. Copy the ready row from the card in STATUS.txt and fill in the time you posted, the link and your name. Format: date and time as 2026-10-28 20:05 (India time); platform exactly as written in the card; no commas in the issue column (use semicolons). **The link:** open the live post, then Instagram: three dots, Link; Facebook: Share, Copy link; LinkedIn: three dots, Copy link to post; X: Share, Copy link. For a Story write "story, no link". **Where to write the log:** open the CSV in a spreadsheet app (for example Google Sheets, File, Import) on your phone or laptop, or use a shared sheet Megh gives you with the same columns. Example row:

```
5,Instagram feed,2026-11-01 20:05,https://www.instagram.com/p/EXAMPLE/,Priya,none
```

**The engagement log.** Add one row per post and platform to **08_ENGAGEMENT_LOG.csv** about 24 hours after the post went out (the next evening, see "The day after"; on Facebook, LinkedIn and X that is one post each day). Columns: post number, platform, posted at, checked at, hours after posting, reach or impressions, likes or reactions, comments, shares, saves, profile visits, new followers, note. Copy each number as the app shows it; leave a cell empty if the app does not show it; never guess. The note is for anything unusual (a complaint, a warning). Instagram shows reach, likes, comments, shares, saves and profile visits under **View insights** (professional account); the other platforms show impressions and reactions on the post. If an Instagram number is missing because the account is not set up for insights, tell Megh.

## Mistakes and how to fix them

| What went wrong | What to do |
|---|---|
| Slides in the wrong order | **Do not delete.** Take a screenshot and tell Megh. Deleting changes the profile picture. |
| A typo in the caption | Edit the caption only (the three dots, then Edit). Paste the right caption from caption.txt. Do not touch the pictures. Tell Megh. |
| The wrong picture went up | **Do not delete.** Screenshot, tell Megh. |
| You posted something marked STOP | Tell Megh at once. Do not delete until Megh says so. |
| You forgot alt text | Add it: three dots, Edit, Alt text. |
| A post failed or got stuck | Do not try again many times. Screenshot, tell Megh. |
