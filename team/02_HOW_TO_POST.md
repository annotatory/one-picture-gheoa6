# How to post

Read this once before your first post. Every step names the exact file. Replace "P05" with the post number you are posting. If a button has a slightly different name in your version of an app, pick the closest one and tell Megh.

## Before every post (2 minutes)

1. Open 01_POSTING_CALENDAR. Find today's post. Check the status is **GO** or **GO-WITH-FALLBACK**. If it says **STOP-UNTIL**, do not post. Tell Megh.
2. Open the post folder in 04_POSTS. The folder name ends in the status (for example P05_Focus__GO).
3. Open STATUS.txt. If it says GO-WITH-FALLBACK, note which files it tells you to use.
4. Copy the files to your phone: the pictures for each platform and caption.txt, alt_text.txt. (Share the folder to the phone with AirDrop, Google Drive or a cable.)

## Instagram feed (a carousel of slides)

Files: the folder **INSTAGRAM_feed_4x5**. Names: P05_feed_slide_1_of_3.png, P05_feed_slide_2_of_3.png and so on.

1. Open Instagram. Tap the **+** button, then **Post**.
2. Tap the **Select multiple** button (two overlapping squares), then tap the slides **in order: slide 1 first, then slide 2, then slide 3.** Check the little numbers 1, 2, 3 on the pictures. A post with one slide: just pick it.
3. If Instagram shows a square preview, tap the small format button in the corner of the first picture and choose the **full portrait** (4:5) version, so nothing is cut off.
4. Tap **Next**. Do not add filters, music, stickers or tags. Tap **Next**.
5. **Caption:** open caption.txt, select all, copy. In Instagram tap the caption box and paste. Check nothing is cut off. Do not change a word.
6. **Alt text:** scroll down and tap **Advanced settings**, then **Write alt text**. For each picture, paste the matching line from alt_text.txt (Slide 1 for picture 1, and so on). Tap **Done**.
7. Make sure **Also share to Facebook** is off unless Megh told you otherwise.
8. Tap **Share**.

## Instagram Story

Files: **INSTAGRAM_story_9x16**, the file P05_story.png.

1. Instagram, swipe right (or tap **+** then **Story**).
2. Pick P05_story.png. Do not add text, stickers, music or links unless STATUS.txt says so.
3. Tap **Your story**. A Story has no caption. (The same file can go to WhatsApp Status.)

## LinkedIn (square pictures)

Files: **LINKEDIN_FACEBOOK_1x1**. Names: P05_square_slide_1_of_3.png and so on.

1. Open LinkedIn. Tap **Post** (Start a post).
2. Tap the picture button. Pick the slides **in order, slide 1 first.**
3. Paste caption.txt. Do not change a word.
4. If LinkedIn shows an **Alt text** button when you tap a picture, paste the matching line from alt_text.txt. If it does not, skip this step.
5. Tap **Post**.

## Facebook (square pictures)

Files: **LINKEDIN_FACEBOOK_1x1** (the same files as LinkedIn).

1. Open Facebook. Tap **Photo** in the post box.
2. Pick the slides in order, slide 1 first.
3. Paste caption.txt.
4. If you can edit a photo and see **Alt text**, paste the matching line. If not, skip.
5. Tap **Post**.

## X (one wide picture)

Files: **X_LINKEDIN_wide_16x9**, the file P05_wide.png. Caption: use **caption_for_X.txt** (it is short enough for X).

1. Open X. Tap the new post button.
2. Tap the picture button and pick P05_wide.png.
3. Paste caption_for_X.txt. If there is an alt-text option on the picture (Add description), paste the "Story and wide" line from alt_text.txt.
4. Tap **Post**.

## After you post (every time, every platform)

1. Open the live post on your phone.
2. Swipe through **every slide**. Check the order is 1, 2, 3 and nothing is cropped.
3. Read the caption. Check no word is cut off and the line breaks look right.
4. Check the alt text went in (Instagram: open the post, tap the three dots, Edit, Alt text).
5. Add a line to **07_POSTED_LOG.csv**: post number, platform, date and time, link, your name, and any issue.
6. Instagram only: open the profile page and take a **screenshot of the profile grid**. Send it to Megh.

## Mistakes and how to fix them

| What went wrong | What to do |
|---|---|
| Slides in the wrong order | **Do not delete.** Take a screenshot and tell Megh. Deleting changes the profile picture. |
| A typo in the caption | Edit the caption only (the three dots, then Edit). Paste the right caption from caption.txt. Do not touch the pictures. Tell Megh. |
| The wrong picture went up | **Do not delete.** Screenshot, tell Megh. |
| You posted something marked STOP | Tell Megh at once. Do not delete until Megh says so. |
| You forgot alt text | Add it: three dots, Edit, Alt text. |
| A post failed or got stuck | Do not try again many times. Screenshot, tell Megh. |
