Comments and safety

Nari 1 is a women's health app. People may write personal things. Be warm, short and careful. When in doubt, do not answer: tell Megh.

Answering comments

Do

  • Say thank you. Keep it to one or two lines.
  • Only repeat what the post already says.
  • Tell people the daily check-in and the Chi score are free (that is on the posts).
  • Send questions you cannot answer to Megh.

Never

  • No medical advice. Not even a small tip. No "you should", no "try", no talk about symptoms, tablets, tests or cycles.
  • No diagnosing. Never say what someone "has" or what a symptom "means".
  • No prices other than the published ones: the ₹499 call (only after post 10 is live) and "free" for the daily check-in and the Chi score.
  • No promises. Never promise a launch date, a feature, a result or a reply.
  • Never say the ₹499 call is with a doctor. It is with a Nari health coach or counsellor.
  • No arguing. No emoji storms. No copying other people's comments.

Safe replies you can use word for word (Megh must approve this list before the first use; until then, tell Megh and wait):

  • "Thank you. We are glad it speaks to you."
  • "Thank you for telling us. We have passed it on to the team."
  • "Good question. We will come back to you here." (then tell Megh the same day)

If someone sounds in distress (in a comment or a message)

Examples: she says she wants to hurt herself, she cannot go on, she is in danger.

  1. Reply once, with the helpline lines below and nothing else. Copy them exactly. Do not write your own words, do not add advice, do not add emoji.
  2. Tell Megh straight away (call or message, do not wait for the end of the day). Send a screenshot. Megh's contact is in the "Right now" box at the top of 00_START_HERE.
  3. Do not delete the comment or message. Do not keep chatting. Do not promise anything.

Spam and abuse

  • Spam (ads, links, bots): hide or delete it. No reply.
  • Abuse (insults, harassment): take a screenshot first, then hide the comment. Do not reply. If it continues, block the account and tell Megh.
  • Threats: screenshot, do not reply, tell Megh straight away.
  • A complaint about the app or a post: do not defend, do not explain. Screenshot and tell Megh the same day.

The stop rule

Stop posting and tell Megh before the next post if any of these happens:

  • Comments complain about a post, or several people react badly to the same thing.
  • A platform sends a warning, removes a post, or limits the account.
  • Reach on a post is much lower than on the post before it (as a first rule: less than half). How to check: before you post today's post, open yesterday's Instagram post, tap View insights, and read Accounts reached (this needs a professional account; if you do not see it, tell Megh). Write the number in the issue column of the POSTED_LOG the next day, like "reach 1234". Compare it with the post before. This rule is a default to review after week one, not a result from our own data.
  • Anything in a comment or message that you did not expect or do not understand.
