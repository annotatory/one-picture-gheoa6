One row per post, in the order they go out. Instagram: all 12 go out on the launch day, in one sitting. Nothing goes out until every row says GO or GO-FALLBACK under Status today: if any row says STOP-UNTIL, post nothing. On the launch day, work in rounds: the Instagram feed for posts 1 to 12 first, in order, so the grid forms at once; then Instagram Stories 1 to 12; then post 1 on Facebook, LinkedIn and X. Each day after, the next post on Facebook, LinkedIn and X, never two on one platform in a day. On every platform post n goes out only after post n-1 is live there. If a post fails, gets stuck or the app says to try again later, stop there: never skip a post, never retry many times, tell Megh. Status today is what you may do right now. This post on its own leaves out the start gates and the other 11 posts. Open the post folder named in the Folder column and read STATUS.txt there. After the 12, later posts are paced: Megh sends that plan.
